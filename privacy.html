Privacy – Metabot Logbook

The team engineering notebook of FIRST LEGO League Challenge Team Metabot (#76447). Last updated October 13, 2026.

What this app is

The Metabot Logbook is a private notebook used by one youth robotics team: its team members and their coaches. It is not a public service. Only people who have the page address and a team member's name and code can open it.

What is stored, and where

  • Entries the team writes (session notes, research, robot design notes), the team members' first names, and photos the team uploads of their builds and sketches.
  • Everything is stored in the lead coach's own Google account: one Google Sheet and one Google Drive folder. A Google Apps Script owned by the coach reads and writes them on the team's behalf. The Google permissions the script asks for (Drive, Sheets, connecting to an external service, and running when the coach is not present) are used only for this and for the weekly backup described below.
  • Homework cards from the team's Trello board are read (never changed) so each member can see their tasks.
  • The web page itself is a static file hosted on GitHub Pages. It keeps your sign-in and any entries not yet saved in your browser so that you can keep writing when the connection drops.

What is not collected

  • No analytics, no advertising, no tracking cookies, no accounts and no passwords beyond the team's sign-in codes.
  • No data is sold or shared with anyone outside the team and its coaches. Nothing is sent to third parties other than Google (storage, and the fonts the page uses), GitHub (hosting the page) and Trello (reading homework cards).

Children

The team members are children. Coaches and parents decide what is written and which photos are uploaded; faces are not needed and are discouraged. A parent can ask a coach to remove any entry or photo at any time.

Deleting data

Coaches can delete entries and photos from the logbook. An entry deleted in the logbook is hidden but kept, so that a coach can restore it; a photo a coach removes goes to the Google Drive trash. Once a week an automatic backup copies the sheet and the photos into a folder in the same Google account; the newest 26 copies (about six months) are kept. The whole notebook, including its backups, can be deleted by removing the Google Sheet and Drive folder in the coach's account.

Contact

Questions go to the lead coach, who owns the Google account the data lives in. Team members and parents already have the coach's contact details.
